# Getting started

RedFrontier is set in the Heartlands, around Valentine. This is what you can do in it today.

1. **Open your inventory** with `I`. You carry a hotbar of 5 slots plus a backpack. See [Inventory](systems/fh_inventory.md) and [Controls](controls.md).
2. **Claim a homestead plot.** Stand inside a plot and use `/fh_claim`. You get one plot per character and one owner-only chest on it. See [Homestead](systems/fh_homestead.md).
3. **Clear the outlaw camp.** Heartlands has one outlaw camp. Kill every outlaw and its stash opens with loot. See [Zones](systems/fh_zones.md).
4. **Carry it home.** Some loot is too bulky for the backpack. It goes in a saddlebag, wagon or chest. See [Items](items.md) and [Core](systems/fh_core.md).
5. **If you die**, your hotbar stays with you and your backpack drops in a bag where you fell. Go back for it within 30 minutes. See [Death](systems/fh_death.md).

Hunting pelts exist as items, but skinning and selling are not built yet. See [Hunting](systems/fh_hunting.md).
