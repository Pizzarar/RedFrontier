# Getting started

RedFrontier is set in the Heartlands, around Valentine. This is what you can do in it today. Most of it is built but not yet confirmed in game.

1. **Open your inventory** with `I`. You carry a hotbar of 5 slots plus a backpack. See [Inventory](systems/fh_inventory.md) and [Controls](controls.md).
2. **Claim a homestead plot.** Stand inside a plot and use `/fh_claim`. You get one plot per character and one owner-only chest on it. `/fh_build` with no argument lists what you can build next. See [Homestead](systems/fh_homestead.md).
3. **Look at the jobs board.** `/jobs` opens it from anywhere. It holds 3-5 repeatable offers (hunt a pelt, clear a camp, bounty) and changes every 60 minutes. `/journal` shows your quests. See [Quests](systems/quests.md).
4. **Meet a family.** Three one-time questlines are built (Hale, Marrow, Whitlock). Their givers stand at fixed spots in the Heartlands. Quest rewards are money and reputation.
5. **Hunt.** Skin a dead animal with the "Skin" prompt, then sell the pelt to the trapper with `E`. Big pelts need a wagon within 8 m. See [Hunting](systems/fh_hunting.md).
6. **Clear the outlaw camp.** Heartlands has one outlaw camp. Kill every outlaw and its stash opens with loot. See [Zones](systems/fh_zones.md).
7. **Carry it home.** Bulky loot goes in a saddlebag, wagon or chest. See [Items](items.md).
8. **Eat, drink, rest.** Food, water, rest and cleanliness are timed buffs. If they run out you get sluggish, never hurt. See [Survival](systems/survival.md).
9. **If you die**, your hotbar stays with you and your backpack drops in a bag where you fell. Go back for it within 30 minutes. See [Death](systems/fh_death.md).
