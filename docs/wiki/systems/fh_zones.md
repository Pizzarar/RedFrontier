# Zones: outlaw camps

Outlaw camps are scattered across the four open regions — the Heartlands, Scarlett Meadows, Bayou Nwa and the Great Plains. Each camp belongs to a gang and holds four outlaws.

## How a camp works
1. **Idle.** Nothing is there until you come within 45 m. The camp wakes and four hostile outlaws appear.
2. **Active.** Kill all four.
3. **Cleared.** The stash opens (`E` at the stash, see [Controls](../controls.md)). Loot is rolled when the camp clears.
4. **Respawn.** 10 minutes later the stash is emptied and the camp is idle again. The stash stays open during those 10 minutes.

If the outlaws fail to appear, the camp resets and can't wake for 60 seconds.

## Loot
Clearing an outlaw camp rolls its stash: a **Worn Rifle** (condition 20-45), a **Small Safe**, and usually **Gun Parts** (2-3), plus the odd bit of contraband. The rifle and safe are bulky — bring a saddlebag, wagon or chest (see [Items](../items.md)).

## Camp ownership
Clear a camp and law takes it over; the camp goes quiet until an outlaw squad retakes it 20-30 minutes later. See [A-Life](fh_alife.md).

Camp positions, models and weapons are placeholder guesses that still need checking in game.
