# Zones: outlaw camps

There are two Tier 1 outlaw camps: the Heartlands Outlaw Camp and the Lemoyne Raiders Camp (Scarlett Meadows). See [Regions](../regions.md).

## How a camp works

1. **Idle.** Nothing is there until you come within 45 m. The camp wakes and four hostile outlaws appear.
2. **Active.** Kill all four.
3. **Cleared.** The stash opens (`E` at the stash, see [Controls](../controls.md)). Loot is rolled when the camp clears.
4. **Respawn.** 10 minutes later the stash is emptied, the bodies go and the camp is idle again. The stash stays open during those 10 minutes.

If the outlaws fail to appear, the camp resets and can't wake for 60 seconds.

## Loot

Each clear drops a Worn Rifle (condition 20-45) and a Small Safe. Both are bulky: they don't fit the backpack, so bring a saddlebag, wagon or chest. See [Items](../items.md).

All camp positions, models and weapons are placeholder guesses that still need checking in game.
