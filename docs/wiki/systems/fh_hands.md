# Hired hands

Hire help for your homestead. One kind is built today: the **ranch hand**, who tops up your feed chest.

## The ranch hand
- Keeps your homestead chest stocked with **feed** (up to 40, at most 10 per working tick).
- Earns **$5 per wage tick** (one tick every 10 minutes), charged to your cash.
- If you can't pay 3 ticks in a row, the hand quits.

## Hiring
- Gated behind your Heartlands tier: hiring needs the tier-2 **Hire a hand** unlock (see [Progression](progression.md)).
- Not wired to a player command or menu yet — hiring and firing are server exports today (admin test commands only).
