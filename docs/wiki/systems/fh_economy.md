# Economy: money and reputation

Money you earn is paid in **cash**. Reputation is tracked **per region** (see [Regions](../regions.md)) and can go up or down.

- Every payment and reputation change is written to a permanent ledger.
- A single payout is capped at 100000.
- Reputation per region stays between -10000 and 10000.

Nothing in the game pays you yet apart from admin tools: selling pelts and quest rewards come with later work. See [Hunting](fh_hunting.md).
